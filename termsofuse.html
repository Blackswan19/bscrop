FromBs, From Myootive, and BsDevTol: These services save data in your browser on your device. Data saved only in your browser is not sent to or stored by Black Swan Organization, unless the service clearly tells you that a feature sends data elsewhere.

Browser data may be lost if you clear browser data, reset or remove your browser, use private browsing, change devices, or have a device or browser problem. It may not be available in another browser or on another device.

Black Swan cannot restore data that was saved only in your browser and then deleted or lost because we do not have a copy of it. Check carefully before clearing browser data. Save or export important information first if the service provides that option.

Do not assume that information submitted to these services stays only in your browser.

Sensitive information

We recommend that you do not enter or upload sensitive or private information, such as passwords, bank or card details, identity numbers, medical details, or private documents, unless it is needed for the service and the service clearly explains why it is required.

If you use a shared device or browser, another person with access to it may be able to see data saved there. Keep your device safe and do not share it with people you do not trust.

We take reasonable steps to run our services, but no website, device, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely safe or available at all times. Keep your device secure and save your own copy of important information when possible.
